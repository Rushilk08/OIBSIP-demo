06 — Details

Before you run.

How should I size a Runova?
Most runners size true to their usual street shoe. If you're between sizes, or run trail, we suggest going up half a size for toe room on descents.
Does the carbon plate wear out?
The plate itself doesn't compress like foam does. Expect full performance through 350–450 miles, after which the surrounding foam is the limiting factor.
What's your return policy?
60 days, worn or unworn. Take them on an actual run — if they're not right, send them back for a full refund.
How sustainable is the build?
40% of each pair by weight comes from recycled foam and post-consumer yarn, with a take-back program for full pairs at end of life.
Where do you ship, and how long does it take?
We currently ship to 46 countries. Domestic orders arrive in 2–4 days; international timelines are shown at checkout before you pay.
