Uptime 99.98%

Built for the moment a webhook fails

Endpoints go down. Networks drop packets. Relay assumes failure is normal and handles it for you.

Guaranteed delivery

Failed requests are retried on an exponential backoff schedule for up to 24 hours, so a five-minute outage on your side never costs you data.

Signed payloads

Every request carries an HMAC signature in its headers, so your endpoint can verify it actually came from Relay before it does anything with it.

Live event log

Watch every attempt as it happens: status code, response time, and payload, searchable by endpoint or event type.

Instant replay

Deployed a bug that dropped events on the floor? Pick a time range and resend every affected webhook with one click.

Set up in three steps

No agents to install and nothing to deploy on your side.

  1. 01

    Register an endpoint

    Give Relay the URL that should receive events, and pick which event types it should get.

  2. 02

    Send us an event

    Post JSON to our API, or connect one of our integrations. Relay queues it and starts delivery immediately.

  3. 03

    Monitor and replay

    Track delivery in the dashboard, get alerted on repeated failures, and replay anything that needs a second attempt.

Why we built Relay

We spent years on-call for payment and order systems, and the outages that hurt most were never the big ones — they were a single dropped webhook that quietly desynced two systems for a week. Relay is the piece of infrastructure we wished we had: small, boring, and reliable enough to forget about.

It's used today by teams shipping payments, logistics, and internal tooling who would rather trust a dedicated queue than re-implement retry logic in every service they own.
